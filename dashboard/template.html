World Bank IPF contract awards: procurement dashboard

Source: World Bank, Finances One, dataset DS00005 “Contract Awards in Investment Project Financing (since FY 2020)”, sourced from STEP. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Independent analysis, not a World Bank product.

Caveats: excludes subcontracts and cofinancing. Joint-venture contract values are split equally among members. Supplier country is the country of registration. There is no field for international vs national competition or number of bids. Probable duplicate entries are excluded. The latest fiscal year is partial, and recent months may be under-reported because of data-entry lag.

Definitions: contracts = distinct WB contract numbers; contract value = sum of all supplier rows; “All years” = complete fiscal years only; domestic share = domestic / (domestic + foreign + international organisations); top-10 share = firms only (anonymised individuals excluded).
